# Dallas v2 data reuse

Census ACS 2020–2024 controls, public-use microdata and TIGERweb geography are United States government data. The Census Bureau's [Research Transparency and Public Access policy](https://www2.census.gov/foia/ds_policies/ds027.pdf) describes Census employee works as generally outside United States copyright protection. Cite the Census Bureau, ACS period, geography vintage and table/variable identifiers when reusing these normalized data. PUMS donors describe public-use regional samples; the synthesized records do not identify actual tract residents.

The road database, its retained OpenStreetMap geometry/attributes and OSM-derived access-node linkages are made available under the [Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). **© OpenStreetMap contributors.** See [OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution guidance](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Preserve that attribution and license notice in maps and exports; share adapted road databases under ODbL. The complete normalized road database is included in `../maps/dallas-roads-v2.json`, with its exact public acquisition query and raw-source hash in `../maps/road-admission-v2.json` and the source receipts. Raw responses are retained privately for audit; no map tiles are bundled.

The Census-derived population and the OSM road database remain separately identified layers. Synthetic unit positions, household assignments, facilities and transport speeds are modeled assumptions; they are not Census observations, real addresses, Dallas response resources or present road-condition reports. The cross-layer access-node fields retain the ODbL notice above. This release makes no claim of endorsement by the Census Bureau, City of Dallas or OpenStreetMap.

Each `source-receipts-v2.json` entry records the exact public URL, retrieval time, byte count, raw SHA-256, vintage and reuse notice. Browser fixtures contain normalized small inputs; national/state source bytes and donor serial-number custody stay in `.private/dallas-v2-data/`.
