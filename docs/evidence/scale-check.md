## Evidence limits and participant target

The target is at least 4 of 5 uncoached first-time participants completing the demo goal. **This participant test is unperformed**: no participants were recruited or observed. Automated checks are not user research.

This experiment does not establish browser usability with 1,000 homes, citywide coverage, real household needs, validated triage, concurrent clients, provider performance, deployment capacity or production readiness. Repeated placements and the small fixed resource pool bound what this run demonstrates. Source hashes and exact counters are retained in the private receipt.
